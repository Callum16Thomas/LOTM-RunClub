Club news

SEPTEMBER 20, 2026

Ridge Climb is back on for October

After a summer off while the trailhead lot was under construction, our monthly long route is back on the schedule starting October 12th. Same 7.8-mile route, same water stop at mile 4, same coffee after.

If you haven't done it before: it's our hardest regular route, with two real climbs. Go out easy on the first one — everyone does it too fast the first time.

SEPTEMBER 6, 2026

New runners: here's how Tuesday nights work

We get a lot of the same questions from people showing up for their first run, so here's the short version: meet at the boathouse dock at 6:00, we run the Riverside Easy Loop, and we regroup every mile so nobody gets dropped. Pace doesn't matter. Bring a light if it's already dark where you are.

AUGUST 15, 2026

Club social recap

Thanks to everyone who came out to the pavilion last weekend — good turnout, good food, and we picked up a handful of new regulars for the Tuesday run. If you missed it, we do these a few times a year; check the runs page for the next one.
