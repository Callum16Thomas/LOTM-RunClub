Same river, different mile every week.

We're a free, no-drop-until-you-say-so run club meeting three times a week around the Mystic and Charles rivers. New runners are always welcome — we regroup at every mile.

How it works

Three runs a week

Tuesday nights are easy and social, Thursdays are tempo work, and Sunday mornings are our long run. Show up to whichever fits your week — no sign-up required for regular runs.

Routes for every pace

Every route is tagged by difficulty using our own trail-blaze system: blue for easy, yellow for moderate, red for hard climbs. Check the routes page before you show up so you know what you're in for.
